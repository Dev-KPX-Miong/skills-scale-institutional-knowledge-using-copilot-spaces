# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designers / User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX Designers and User Researchers understand user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, and synthesize findings
- Create user flows, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate usability and accessibility with users
- Translate research findings into actionable product recommendations

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address real user needs
- Make product experiences intuitive and accessible
- Reduce usability risks before release

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and design specifications
- Design reviews and prototype walkthroughs
- Usability findings and accessibility feedback

### Collaboration
- Partner with Product Managers to inform discovery, priorities, and acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility and clarify design intent during implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility in validation
- Share research insights with Project Managers and Stakeholders to support decisions and alignment

---

## Tech Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Architects guide technical direction and help ensure solutions are secure, maintainable, and feasible.

### Responsibilities
- Define or guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and integration dependencies
- Support Developers with decomposition, estimation, and technical reviews
- Document significant decisions and communicate their implications

### Goals
- Deliver reliable and maintainable technical solutions
- Surface technical trade-offs early
- Enable consistent implementation across the team

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design proposals
- Technical risk and dependency updates
- Design reviews and technical decision records

### Collaboration
- Work with Developers on implementation choices, code reviews, and technical problem-solving
- Coordinate with Project Managers on dependencies, estimates, and delivery risks
- Explain technical options and trade-offs to Product Managers and Stakeholders
- Partner with QA/Testing on testability, integration risks, and quality expectations

---

## DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Site Reliability Engineers support reliable delivery and operation of services through automation and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain deployment automation and CI/CD practices
- Define observability, reliability, and operational readiness needs
- Plan capacity, resilience, and recovery approaches with the team
- Support deployment verification and incident response

### Goals
- Make releases repeatable and dependable
- Detect and resolve service issues quickly
- Improve service reliability and operational visibility

### Typical Communication
- Deployment and operational readiness updates
- Reliability metrics, alerts, and incident reports
- Runbooks and infrastructure or automation changes

### Collaboration
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing on automation, environment needs, and release validation
- Coordinate with Project Managers on operational dependencies, release readiness, and incident escalation
- Advise Product Managers and Stakeholders about reliability risks and operational impact
- Work with Security / Privacy Leads on secure infrastructure and operational controls

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads help teams identify and address security, privacy, and compliance needs throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on security and privacy requirements and applicable controls
- Facilitate threat and privacy risk assessments
- Review designs and changes for security or privacy concerns
- Guide remediation and escalation of material risks

### Goals
- Protect users, data, and services
- Identify security and privacy risks early
- Ensure risks and required controls are visible and addressed

### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy requirements or review findings
- Risk assessments and remediation guidance
- Escalations for significant risks or incidents

### Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers to incorporate security and privacy needs into requirements and priorities
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to validate controls and test security-sensitive behavior
- Coordinate with Tech Leads / Architects on secure designs and technical mitigations
- Help Project Managers track risks, owners, and escalations; inform Stakeholders when decisions or action are needed
- Coordinate with DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers on infrastructure and operational controls

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations Representatives bring customer-facing and service-operation needs into planning, release readiness, and feedback loops.

### Responsibilities
- Identify support workflows, customer impact, and common service needs
- Prepare support guidance, escalation paths, and readiness materials
- Share customer feedback and recurring issues with the project team
- Help triage customer-impacting issues after release

### Goals
- Prepare support teams and customers for changes
- Resolve customer issues effectively
- Ensure feedback informs product improvements

### Typical Communication
- Support readiness checklists and knowledge-base updates
- Customer feedback and issue trends
- Release communications and escalation reports

### Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers to share customer needs and feedback that may inform priorities
- Coordinate with Project Managers on release readiness, support communications, and escalation paths
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to reproduce and triage customer-reported issues
- Share operational and customer impact with Stakeholders and DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers

---

## Applying These Roles

These personas describe responsibilities, not mandatory separate positions. Teams can combine or adapt roles to fit project size and context, while ensuring each responsibility has a clear owner and the relevant collaborators are involved.

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
